---
title: Shipping is a feature
lede: The moment a change reaches real use is when its design begins, not when the ticket closes.
---

It is easy to treat release work as the final administrative step of product work. In practice, a release changes what someone can do. That makes shipping a part of the feature.

## Make the first use intentional

The first screen, first empty state, and first error after a release decide whether a change feels finished. A good handoff gives people context and a clear path forward.

## Learn while the work is still fresh

Release notes, support questions, and a small amount of instrumentation are often more useful than another planning meeting. They show where a mental model did not transfer from the team to the user.

The output is not simply deployed code. It is a working feedback loop.
