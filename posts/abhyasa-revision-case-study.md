This sample case study describes an Abhyasa product direction. It is a narrative example, not a claim of measured learning outcomes.

## The problem

After finishing a lesson, a learner still needs to decide what to revisit. A list of chapters can show everything available without making the next useful step clear.

## The approach

The learning experience can place a recommended revision step beside the learner's current chapter and progress. The recommendation should explain why the chapter is being suggested and let the learner continue in context.

## What to validate

The team would look at whether learners understand the recommendation, whether they start a revision session, and whether the suggestion remains useful as progress changes.

## Current state

The project update describes work on this direction. This case study is a sample of how the decision and its outcome could be documented once observed.
