---
title: Case study sample: a delayed shipment agent
lede: A working concept for helping warehouse teams investigate delayed shipments with clear evidence and a human decision point.
---

This is a sample case study for an in-progress Vegath project. It describes the problem and the intended approach; it does not report a deployed result or measured impact.

## The problem

A delayed shipment can involve inventory, a handoff, a carrier, or an incomplete record. Finding the cause often means opening several systems before a team member can decide what to do next.

## The proposed workflow

The agent would collect shipment context, identify exceptions, and draft a concise investigation summary. Deterministic rules would handle known checks. Any suggested action that changes an operational record would remain visible for human review.

## What the case study would measure

The useful measures are time to a supported explanation, the share of recommendations accepted by operators, and the rate of incorrect or incomplete summaries. Those results are still to be established.

## Current state

This is an in-progress concept. The next step is to test it against realistic delayed-shipment examples and document where it helps or fails.
