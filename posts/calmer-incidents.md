---
title: A calmer approach to incident response
lede: Incidents become manageable when the team reduces uncertainty one question at a time.
---

Production incidents are stressful because the system is changing while everyone is trying to understand it. Calm is not a personality trait; it comes from a shared process.

## Narrate observable facts

Start with timestamps, affected behavior, and the last known good state. Separate these facts from guesses. A simple timeline keeps a team from repeating the same investigation in parallel.

## Choose a safe next action

Rollback, rate limit, or disable the smallest risky path. The best next action is usually the one that makes the system safer while giving the team more time to learn.

Afterward, turn the missing signal or unsafe default into a small piece of engineering work. That is how an incident pays forward.
