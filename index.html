Experience

Ten years building systems that work at scale.

At Delhivery and Ula, I built backend platforms where throughput, correctness, and reliability had direct consequences for teams in the field. That engineering foundation now informs how I design and evaluate AI agents.

Delhivery

From pickup to dispatch and payouts

I worked on first-mile platforms handling 5M+ shipments a day, a payouts platform for 100K+ users, and AI-driven dispatch. These systems connected routing, exception handling, operational decisions, and financial flows at scale.

Ula

Procurement, inventory, and warehouse flow

I built procurement and warehouse systems, improved inventory accuracy through inter-warehouse transfers, and developed order throttling infrastructure. The work required dependable data flows and a clear understanding of how stock and orders move.
