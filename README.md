## Experience

### Ten years building systems that work at scale.

At Delhivery and Ula, I built backend platforms where **throughput, correctness, and reliability** had direct consequences for teams in the field. That engineering foundation now informs how I design and evaluate AI agents.

**Delhivery — From pickup to dispatch and payouts.** I worked on first-mile platforms handling **5M+ shipments a day**, a payouts platform for **100K+ users**, and AI-driven dispatch. These systems connected routing, exception handling, operational decisions, and financial flows at scale.

**Ula — Procurement, inventory, and warehouse flow.** I built procurement and warehouse systems, improved inventory accuracy through inter-warehouse transfers, and developed order throttling infrastructure. The work required dependable data flows and a clear understanding of how stock and orders move.

Today I apply that experience to **reliable AI agents**: defining useful evals, measuring inference performance, benchmarking model behavior, and improving LLM serving with techniques such as **KV caching**. I care about what an agent does under real operating conditions, not just in a demo.
